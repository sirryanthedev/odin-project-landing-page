Welcome to Purr & Pour

Where coffee craftsmanship meets feline companionship.

Our Mission: Purr & Pour was founded with two simple goals in mind: to brew exceptional artisan coffee and to give rescue cats a safe, loving environment while they await their forever homes. Every cup of coffee you enjoy directly supports our local partner shelters.

How it works: Our cafe is split into two areas: a modern lounge where our expert baristas prepare drinks and treats, and a cozy cat play space where you can relax, study, or socialize with our resident felines.
