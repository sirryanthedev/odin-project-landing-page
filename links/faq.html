Frequently Asked Questions

  1. Do I need a reservation?
    • Answer: Walk-ins are always welcome in our coffee lounge! However, to ensure a calm environment for our cats, capacity in the cat room is limited, so we highly recommend booking a table online in advance for weekends.
  2. Can I bring my own pet?
    • Answer: For the health, safety, and comfort of our resident cats, outside animals are strictly not permitted inside the cafe.
  3. Are all of the cats adoptable?
    • Answer: Yes! All of our furry hosts come from local animal rescue organizations and are fully vaccinated, spayed/neutered, and ready to go to loving forever homes.
